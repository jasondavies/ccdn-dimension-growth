Full-paper formalisation obligations
===================================

Scope: Castryck, Cluckers, Dittmann and Nguyen, "The dimension growth
conjecture, polynomial in the degree and without logarithmic factors",
Algebra & Number Theory 14 (2020), 2261-2294.
Primary source: https://rcluckers.perso.math.cnrs.fr/prints/Cast-Cl-Ditt-Nguy.pdf
DOI: https://doi.org/10.2140/ant.2020.14.2261

The task covers the remainder of the paper, including its class-group
application. It is not complete when only the affine curve theorem is
proved. Definitions of targets and theorems conditional on missing
mathematics are not completed counting theorems.

Main statements
---------------

Theorem 1: PROVED. projective_dimension_growth_cubic gives, for every ambient
n, a positive C(n), uniformly over homogeneous prime coordinate ideals of
degree d>=5 and real B>=1:
  N(X,B) <= C(n)*d^(2*n^3)*B^dim(X).
Degree and dimension are determined by the actual filtered Hilbert polynomial.
The proof constructs bounded finite birational projection, a homogeneous
irreducible image equation of exactly degree d, actual finite fibers and
height control. Zero-dimensional and empty loci are included. No geometric,
finite-field or counting theorem is supplied as an extra premise.
The earlier projective_dimension_growth endpoint retains exponent
18+10(n+1)+2(n+1)^2; the hypersurface endpoint retains 18+9n in P^(n+2).

Theorem 2: PROVED. AffineCurveCounting.projectiveCurveBound proves
ProjectiveCurveBound: for each n>=2, one C(n)>0 bounds the actual rational
projective count by C(n)*d^4*B^(2/d), uniformly over prime coordinate ideals,
positive homogeneous Hilbert degree d and real B>=1. The coordinate ideal
and degree are independent of the height radius. The constant depends only
on n; there is no epsilon or logarithmic loss, and no supplied projection,
geometric-integrality, finite-field-count or coefficient-height hypothesis.
The proof includes exact projective/affine chart Hilbert-function equality,
bounded degree-preserving projections, all exceptional fibers, points at
infinity, and height-preserving coordinate permutations.
Check: scripts/check.sh; Axioms.lean also prints the endpoint's axioms.

Theorem 3: PROVED. AffineCurveCounting.affineCurveBound proves the full
ambient-dimensional affine-curve bound, with degree specified by eventual
affine Hilbert growth:
N_aff(X,B) <= C(n) d^3 B^(1/d) (log B + d).
Check: scripts/check.sh; declaration and transitive-axiom audit in Axioms.lean.

Theorem 4: PROVED. For each n > 2, constants c(n), e(n), uniformly for
integer polynomials of degree d >= 5 with absolutely irreducible leading
homogeneous part (irreducible over Q-bar, as in the rendered paper):
N_aff(f,B) <= c(n) d^e(n) B^(n-2), B >= 1.
top_form_hypersurface_dimension_growth_cubic gives C(n)*d^(18+3n)*B^(n+1)
for n+3 integer variables and every d>=5, using hyperplane normals bounded
by d^3. In ambient dimension N this is the corrected exponent 3N+9. The small-degree surface cover,
coefficient-height reduction, hyperplanes, slice equations and count injections
are constructed; no missing geometric/counting premise. The rational-equation
version is also proved.

Proposition 5: PROVED. For every integer d > 0, construct an integral
projective plane curve X of degree d and integer B >= 1 for which
(1/5) d^2 B^(2/d) <= N(X,B).
projective_integral_curve_lower_bound constructs the actual homogeneous
equation, proves absolute irreducibility, prime defining ideal, and exact
projective Hilbert degree, and counts the actual projective zero locus.

Theorem 6: PROVED. classGroup_two_torsion_bound chooses one positive C(n)
for every degree n>=3, before the number field, and proves
  h_2(K) <= C(n)*|disc(K)|^(1/2-1/(2n))*(log|disc(K)|)^(1-(n mod 2)).
OddDegreeClassGroupBound supplies the logarithm-free odd-degree branch.
NoIndexTwoClassGroupBound counts actual norm curves and exceptional subfields
when no index-two intermediate field exists. QuadraticClassGroupDescent proves
an actual one-step bound via unit obstructions, invariant ideal classes and
a finite discriminant-prime cover. Exact discriminant descent and proved
prime-factor/polylogarithmic subpower estimates allow strong induction on the
field degree. Both small-degree base cases are proved. No external genus,
lattice, curve-counting or class-group theorem is an additional premise.

Section 6 consequences: PROVED. Actual lower-bound families establish the
stated obstructions: projective_curve_factor_not_isLittleO excludes o(d^2)
in the B^(2/d) curve envelope; affine_curve_log_factor_not_isLittleO excludes
o(d^2/log d) in the B^(1/d)*log B envelope.
projective_hypersurface_degree_exponent_ge_one covers every projective
ambient dimension >=2; affine_hypersurface_degree_exponent_ge_two covers
every affine ambient dimension >=3. The affine examples satisfy the
absolutely irreducible leading-form hypothesis of Theorem 4.

Infrastructure and proof routes
------------------------------

ProjectiveBox: bounded projective-height sets are finite, actual projective
point counts are defined, and primitive integer coordinates give exactly
the maximum-coordinate height in the paper.
ProjectivePrimitive: construct primitive integer representatives and prove
their injectivity as a chosen map and their height bounds.
ProjectiveZeroLocus: homogeneous evaluation scales by the degree; projective
zero loci are independent of representatives; finite projective counts
reduce to bounded primitive integer families.
ProjectivePointSeparation: distinct projective points have homogeneous
Kronecker-delta separators in every degree at least the number of points
minus one.
ProjectiveSection: degree via the actual graded Hilbert function, uniqueness
of degree, and a sharp dm bound for a proper degree-m section of a prime
curve with Hilbert degree d.
ProjectivePlaneBezout: a nonzero homogeneous plane equation of degree d has
projective Hilbert degree d; an irreducible plane equation and a homogeneous
nonmultiple of degree m have at most dm common projective rational points,
including points at infinity.
The zero-locus, interpolation, section and plane Bezout statements now hold
over any field, including the finite fields needed by the determinant method.

SingularApproximation, SingularLocalDeterminant and SingularPlaneChart:
the existing exact-zero theorems are extended to zeros modulo the required
prime power. The original theorem interfaces remain available as consequences.
ProjectiveLocalDeterminant: integer unit normalization modulo prime powers
transfers the singular affine determinant exponent to homogeneous evaluations,
in every coordinate chart; row-block exponents add in every maximal minor.
ProjectiveReduction: primitive integer vectors reduce to actual nonzero
projective points; the residue points determine a unit coordinate, integer
chart lifts and their affine congruences, and preserve the homogeneous equation.
ProjectiveResidueDeterminant: the actual residue partition and positive
multiplicities are constructed from a nonzero homogeneous reduction. Their
sum gives the quadratic single-prime determinant bound, including singular
classes and points outside the standard affine chart.
ProjectiveAffineChart, ProjectiveResidueCardinality: the standard affine
chart is injective; an irreducible reduced homogeneous curve not containing
the line at infinity has at most d points there. Its projective point count
is bounded by the affine chart count plus d.
HomogeneousChartIrreducible: dehomogenization preserves degree and
irreducibility when the coordinate does not divide the equation. For
irreducible homogeneous equations of degree >= 2, the coordinate cannot
divide the equation or be divisible by it.
ProjectiveFiniteFieldCount: for d >= 2, irreducible homogeneous reduction,
and p > 27*d^4, the actual projective residue count is at most
p + 162*d^2*sqrt(p). No finite-field point-count theorem is left as a
hypothesis in this result.
ProjectiveMultiplicity: residues of multiplicity >=2 inject chartwise into
singular affine zeros. There are at most 3*d*(d-1) such residues, giving
total multiplicity <= #ProjectivePlaneResidue + 3*d^2*(d-1).
ProjectivePrimeDeterminant: total multiplicity <= p+165*d^2*sqrt(p) above
the fourth-power cutoff; local divisors sum to the sharp global logarithmic
minor-gcd lower bound.
HomogeneousChartIrreducible also proves that full-degree chart irreducibility
implies homogeneous irreducibility. ProjectiveHeightDeterminant uses this
to discharge good reductions and the bad-prime charge through the actual
coefficient-controlled witness. No local counting input remains.
ProjectiveHeightComparison, ProjectiveAuxiliary: exact Hilbert rank and
Bombieri--Vaaler give actual homogeneous auxiliary polynomials with explicit
degree bounds in both coefficient-height ranges, for all primitive points.
ProjectiveAuxiliaryCount: the auxiliary nonmultiple cuts out at most d*M
projective points by the proved projective Bezout theorem.
ProjectiveTranslation: bounded integer shears preserve primitive vectors,
projective distinctness, homogeneous equations and absolute irreducibility;
the height radius grows by at most d+1.
ProjectivePlanePositionedCount, ProjectivePlaneAbsoluteCount: coefficient
positioning removes the coefficient-height dependence; primitive normalization
transfers the bound to arbitrary absolutely irreducible rational equations.
ProjectivePlaneNonabsoluteCount: extension splitting gives at most d^2+d
base-field projective points, using the affine nonabsolute bound and Bezout
at infinity. This discharges rational irreducibility without geometric
irreducibility.
ProjectivePlaneLinearCount: Schwartz--Zippel bounds linear equations by
9*B^2. projective_plane_equation_bound combines all cases into the uniform
C*d^4*B^(2/d) plane-equation bound for every positive degree.
ProjectiveChartDegree: normalization in the coordinate fraction field
identifies each homogeneous coordinate piece with the corresponding filtered
piece of the affine chart, giving exact Hilbert-function equality in every
degree. ProjectiveChartIdeal: the actual chart ideal is prime, has the same
degree, and contains the images of all projective points in the chart.
ProjectiveIntegerHeight: arbitrary integral representatives bound the
projective height; primitive normalization only decreases the coordinate box.
ProjectivePlaneProjection: the proved bounded affine projection lifts to a
projective plane map with the same polynomial height factor.
ProjectiveProjectionCount: the plane equation has exactly degree d; projection
loses at most d(d-1) points on the affine chart and d on the omitted hyperplane.
ProjectivePermutation: reindexing coordinates preserves height and every
homogeneous Hilbert dimension, allowing a proper chart whenever points exist.
ProjectiveCurveBound: absorb the projection scale into a constant depending
only on ambient dimension and assemble the full Theorem 2.

BoundedDifferentialConstants begins the positive-characteristic criterion:
zero derivative and reduced numerator/denominator degrees below the
characteristic imply that a rational function is constant. For an integral
algebraic differential constant, the same bounds on every coefficient of
its actual minimal polynomial imply descent to the algebraically closed
base. These degree bounds are EXPLICIT INPUTS. For closed-form residues,
the following proved direct elimination argument avoids those inputs.

DifferentialConstantRelation proves that t'=1 prevents a nonzero polynomial
in t of degree below the characteristic, with differential-constant
coefficients, from vanishing. A nonzero bivariate relation with that degree
bound therefore forces any differential-constant second argument to descend
to the algebraically closed base.

g(beta)/f'(beta). Here m is the outer degree of f and D bounds the parameter
degrees of the coefficients of f and g; g has smaller outer degree.
